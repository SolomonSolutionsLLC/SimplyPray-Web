Privacy Policy

We built SimplyPray to be a private place for prayer. Here is a plain-English account of what data we collect, what we do not collect, and why.

Effective date: October 2, 2026  ·  Company: Solomon Solutions LLC

The short version: Your prayers and journal entries stay on your device — we never see them. We collect your email address to create your account, and subscription status to grant access to paid features. We do not run ads or sell your data. The SimplyPray app does not include a third-party analytics SDK. Separately, this public marketing website offers optional Google Analytics only after you choose Accept analytics; see section 8.

1. What we collect

Data Where it lives Why we collect it
Email address & hashed password Supabase servers Account creation and sign-in
Supabase user ID Supabase servers Ties your account to subscription entitlements and any shared content you create
Subscription status Supabase servers Determines which features you can access
Shared prayer list content Supabase servers Only content you explicitly post to a shared group list — never your private prayers
Prayer requests, journal, answered-prayer log, tags, streaks Your device only (SwiftData) Your private prayer data. It never leaves your device and is not uploaded anywhere.
In-app usage events (e.g. "prayer marked prayed") Your device only (SwiftData) Local-only analytics for streak tracking and app improvement. Not transmitted off-device.

No iCloud sync is active at launch. Your private prayer data is local to the device you installed the app on.

2. Your prayers stay on your device

Prayer requests, confession prompts, thanksgiving entries, journal notes, and answered-prayer records are stored exclusively in SwiftData on your iPhone. We have no server-side copy of this content. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

The app works fully offline. No background sync occurs while the app is running.

3. Account data (Supabase)

When you create an account, your email address and a hashed password are stored in Supabase (our authentication provider). We store the minimum needed to let you sign in and manage your subscription.

Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from within the app (tap the account icon → Delete Account). Doing so permanently deletes your account record and any server-side data associated with it via the delete_account Supabase function. There is no waiting period.

4. Shared lists and groups

SimplyPray includes an opt-in shared-list feature (church groups, family prayer lists, etc.). Only content you explicitly post to a shared list is stored on our servers. This is strictly opt-in — your private prayer data is never affected. Shared-list content is protected by row-level security in Supabase so only members of your group can read it.

5. Subscriptions and payments

SimplyPray offers a paid subscription tier. Payments are processed by:

  • Apple In-App Purchase — for purchases made inside the iOS app. Apple handles all payment processing. We never see your card number or billing details. Apple notifies us of your subscription status.
  • Stripe — for web checkout at app.simplypray.io. Stripe handles all card processing and stores payment-method data. We store only the resulting subscription status in Supabase.

We store subscription status (active, cancelled, tier) to determine which features you can access. We do not store card numbers, CVVs, or full billing addresses.

6. No ad tracking

The SimplyPray app does not use advertising or third-party tracking. Optional analytics on this public marketing website is separate from the app and is not used for advertising.

  • NSPrivacyTracking = false — declared in the app's privacy manifest. No cross-app or cross-site tracking occurs.
  • No third-party analytics SDK (e.g. Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude) is included in the app.
  • The in-app analytics service tracks events locally (streaks, usage patterns) and persists them only to your device's SwiftData store. Nothing is transmitted to us or to any third party.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with data brokers or advertising networks.

7. Email communications

We send transactional emails to the address on your account. These include:

  • Account creation and email verification
  • Password reset requests
  • Subscription confirmation, renewal, and cancellation notices

These emails are sent from no-reply@simplypray.io via Amazon Simple Email Service (SES). We do not send marketing or promotional emails without your explicit consent.

8. Cookies and the public website

The public marketing website at www.simplypray.io offers optional Google Analytics 4 to help us understand visits to the home, privacy, and product-support pages. No Google Analytics script, analytics cookie, or analytics request is used before you choose Accept analytics. Choosing Reject analytics keeps it off. Browser Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track preferences also keep it off.

If you accept, Google receives fixed public-page names and canonical addresses, basic visit and device information, and pseudonymous identifiers stored in first-party analytics cookies. Network requests also expose your IP address to Google for processing. We do not send URL query strings, fragments, referring-page addresses, form entries, email addresses, account IDs, prayer requests, journals, shared-list content, or counseling information. ACTS preview pages and the SimplyPray app are excluded.

We do not use this analytics setup for advertising, remarketing, Google Signals, user-provided data collection, or automatic measurement of forms, links, searches, scrolling, videos, or downloads. Google Analytics may generate basic session and engagement events after you accept.

Your analytics choice is stored in this browser for up to 180 days. If you accept, the _ga and stream-specific _ga_… cookies also last up to 180 days without automatic renewal. These cookies are limited to this website host and are not shared with app.simplypray.io. You can change or withdraw consent using Analytics settings on the home, privacy, or support page. Rejecting after accepting stops analytics and clears this integration’s cookies. We reload to unload the Google tag only when browser storage confirms that analytics will stay off. If your browser prevents saving or removing the old choice, analytics remains disabled on the current page; clear this site’s stored data before reloading or leaving the page to prevent that old choice from being reused. It does not delete visit data already sent to Google. If browser storage is unavailable, your choice applies only to the current page.

This site also loads Google Fonts independently of the optional analytics choice. For more about Google’s processing, see Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

The web app at app.simplypray.io uses essential cookies required for authentication. Specifically, Supabase stores a session token in your browser so you stay signed in. These cookies are necessary for the app to function and are not used for advertising or tracking purposes.

10. Data retention

We retain your account data (email, user ID, subscription status) for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, server-side data is permanently deleted.

On-device data (prayers, journal, usage events) is deleted when you delete the app, or when you clear app data on your device.

Local usage-event records are automatically pruned after 90 days by the app.

11. Children

SimplyPray is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has provided us their information, contact us at the address below.

12. Your rights and choices

Regardless of where you live, you can:

  • Access the email address and subscription status we hold for your account by signing in.
  • Delete your account at any time from inside the app (tap the account icon → Delete Account). This permanently deletes your server-side data.
  • Withdraw public-website analytics consent using Analytics settings, then Reject analytics.
  • Delete on-device data by deleting the app from your iPhone.
  • Opt out of transactional emails by deleting your account (we have no marketing list to unsubscribe from).

To request information about data we hold on you, or if you have any other privacy question, contact us at joshua@solomonsolutions.tech.

13. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we may also notify you by email. Continued use of SimplyPray after a change takes effect constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
