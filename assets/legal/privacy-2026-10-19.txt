SimplyPray Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

1 Scope and who we are

Solomon Solutions LLC, a Colorado limited liability company doing business as SimplyPray ("SimplyPray," "we," "us," or "our"), provides personal and church devotional tools. This Policy describes our handling of personal information through our SimplyPray app, related websites, account and sharing services, and communications. This Policy covers supported Apple and Android versions, including distribution through Apple’s App Store and Google Play, and our related websites. SimplyPray is currently offered to users in the United States. Availability differs by platform and app version.

Personal devotional records stay on your device unless you enable optional iCloud Sync or separately share or send them through an online feature. Private iCloud sync and sharing with other people are separate choices. We also process information for accounts, purchases, shared prayer lists, church participation, websites and support. Joining a church does not give it access to your private records. Churches and other recipients are responsible for their independent use of information you share with them.

Platform differences. Optional iCloud Sync is available only on supported Apple devices where the feature is offered and enabled. It does not sync private devotional records to Android. The Android app does not currently provide personal cloud sync. Signing in to the same SimplyPray account on Apple and Android does not by itself copy your private prayers, journals or devotional progress between them. Account, subscription and deliberately shared information use their separate online services.

2 Private devotional records and optional iCloud sync

Your private devotional records, including prayers, confession and thanksgiving entries, journals, tags and devotional activity, are stored on your device. A cloud copy is created only if you enable optional iCloud Sync or separately share or send information through another online feature. An internet connection is required for initial account setup and verification of your agreement to the published policies. After setup and verified agreement acceptance, private devotional features can be used offline on that device, subject to the applicable subscription, trial or church-sponsored access requirements. Account, purchase, sharing and optional sync functions require an internet connection when they communicate with online services. When the app detects a newer required policy version, you must review and accept it online before continuing.

Optional iCloud Sync. Where available in your app version, sync starts turned off and requires your separate choice on each device for each SimplyPray account. Enabling it uploads existing and future personal prayer records to Apple’s private CloudKit storage. Synced information includes prayer titles and details, ACTS categories including personal confession and thanksgiving entries, personal lists, tags, journals and answer notes, answered-prayer history, prayer dates and counts, reading progress, and completion-day and streak information. Account and record identifiers, timestamps and change information are used to keep records associated with the correct account and to combine updates and apply deletions.

Use the same SimplyPray and iCloud accounts on each supported Apple device. Sync combines separate records from those devices. Competing edits to the same prayer, list or journal use the version with the most recent change time; both versions are not kept. Device clock accuracy matters. The most recent intentional reading-day choice is used, and completion days are combined without counting the same calendar day twice. Sync checks while the app is active and waits during an open prayer flow; updates are not necessarily immediate.

Private iCloud sync does not give church administrators or other SimplyPray users access to your personal records and does not put those records in our account or shared-list database. Protect the devices signed in to your accounts. Shared lists use their separate online service. Reminder settings, Bible version preferences, credentials, subscription information, the local devotional activity log and unfinished ACTS checkboxes are not included in the personal sync copy.

You can turn sync off in the app on any device. That device stops syncing, but records already on it and in iCloud remain. Other devices with sync enabled can continue syncing. Turning sync on again combines local records with the existing cloud copy. Turning sync off, signing out, uninstalling the app and deleting your account are different actions. Sections 10 and 11 explain retention and deletion.

Apple device-provider backups or transfers may include your app data, depending on your settings. The Android app disables automatic app cloud backup and excludes its private preferences from the configured backup and device-transfer mechanisms. Device manufacturers and operating-system versions may handle transfers differently; do not treat this setting as a guarantee that no device-level copy can exist. Files you deliberately export or save through a document provider follow that provider’s storage and backup settings. The device provider’s settings and policies govern those services. SimplyPray cannot restore records kept only on your device from its servers. Optional sync depends on access to the applicable accounts, a working connection, compatible app versions and available iCloud storage. It is not a complete backup or a guarantee that every lost record or earlier version can be recovered.

The Apple app keeps a local devotional activity log, including event information and tag names. Events older than approximately 90 days are pruned when the app runs its cleanup routine. This is not a retention limit for all prayers, journals, saved tags, streaks or device settings. The log is not sent to a third-party analytics service.

Your private records are stored in a separate on-device profile for your account. Signing out does not delete them. Account deletion and copies in iCloud, on other devices or in backups are explained in Section 11. Protect your device and review its sharing, transfer and backup settings.

3 Information handled by online services

Account information. We collect your email address, display name, account identifier and authentication information when you create or manage an account. Our authentication provider handles account authentication, passwords and tokens. If you choose third-party sign-in where offered, the sign-in provider and our authentication service process information needed to authenticate and connect your account. We do not need your private devotional records to create an account.

Purchases and access. We and our subscription providers process account identifiers, subscription and purchase information, transaction identifiers and, where needed, your email address to provide and manage paid or church-sponsored access. The app marketplace processes purchases under its own terms; we do not receive your full payment-card number. Website or church billing, where offered, identifies the billing provider and purchase terms at checkout. In the iOS app, RevenueCat also receives device and technical information, including the device’s identifier for vendor, app and operating-system information, and interactions with subscription offer screens, such as views and purchase actions. This supports subscription access, reporting and analytics; it does not include the text of your private prayers or journals.

Android purchases. Where Google Play purchases are offered, Google processes payment and purchase information under its own terms and privacy policy. We and our subscription administration provider, RevenueCat, receive app user identifiers, purchase history and transaction or purchase-token information needed to validate purchases, administer access, and provide subscription reporting and analytics. This does not send the text of your private prayers or journals to Google Play or RevenueCat. The iOS identifier-for-vendor disclosure above is specific to Apple devices; it does not describe an Android identifier. Other Android data practices depend on the features actually offered and are described in the corresponding sections of this Policy.

Shared content and membership. If you use sharing or church features, we process the list name and description, prayer request title and text, status and moderation information, submitting user identifier, timestamps, membership and role information, invitations and related settings. Inviting someone by email sends that address and invitation information to our services even if the recipient does not yet have an account.

Safety and agreement records. Online services record abuse reports and their details, reported users and requests, block relationships, moderation actions and outcomes, and related timestamps. We also keep versioned agreement receipts linked to your account, including the exact policy versions and recorded acceptance time, to verify your agreement and provide your agreement history. These records are separate from your private devotional activity log.

Church activity. When you are an active church member, app activity can update an activity date held by our service. Church administrators receive a yes/no indicator of whether activity was recorded within the past 60 days, rather than an exact last-active timestamp. This indicates app activity, not the text of private prayers or proof that you completed a prayer session.

Support and communications. We receive information you send us, including your email address, message and any attachments. Please omit private devotional or third-party details that are unnecessary to resolve your question. If you voluntarily send such information, it will be handled through the support channel rather than remaining only on your device.

Website and newsletter information. Our websites and embedded services receive technical request information, such as IP address and browser or device information. Newsletter signup collects your email address and first name when supplied through our newsletter service. The website loads externally served fonts, which requires your browser to send technical request information to the font provider. Hosting, email and network-delivery services also process information needed to deliver the service. If you accept optional analytics on our public website, the analytics provider also receives the visit information described in Section 9.

We use information to create and secure accounts; authenticate users; provide optional private iCloud sync when you request it; provide and administer subscriptions and church-sponsored access; deliver invitations and shared content to its intended audience; administer memberships and moderation; respond to support and privacy requests; maintain service reliability and security; prevent misuse; and meet legal obligations. We use church membership activity information to support administration of church participation.

When you request newsletters, launch updates or other optional email resources, we use your contact information for those communications. We use technical request information to operate and secure websites and services. Our subscription providers process account identifiers and purchase information to validate purchases, manage subscription access, and provide subscription reporting and analytics. We do not send private devotional records to those providers for analytics. We do not use advertising pixels or third-party crash-reporting services in our app, web app or marketing site. The public marketing website offers optional website analytics to help us understand visits, only after you accept analytics as described in Section 9. We do not use private devotional records for advertising or send them through this website analytics integration. Local logging, church activity and provider operational processing remain separate activities.

We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it for targeted advertising. We do not use shared prayer content to build advertising profiles. Where law requires consent for a particular use or disclosure of sensitive information, we obtain that consent separately; accepting general terms or this Policy does not replace required consent.

5 Sharing choices and other people’s information

Shared requests are stored on our server and may be read by authorized participants, administrators and service personnel. Church administrators can view member names, email addresses, join dates and a yes/no indicator of app activity within the past 60 days, and download the roster. The indicator does not show an exact last-active time or prove that you prayed. Administrators can review published, pending and retained requests as their permissions allow. They cannot access your private records, whether kept only on your device or privately synced through iCloud, unless you separately share or send them. The church appoints its privacy contact and authorized list administrators.

Church prayer lists are private and require current active church membership and qualifying church-sponsored access. A personal subscription alone does not grant access to a church’s lists. Church lists do not have public pages, open joining or separate per-list invitations. History access depends on permissions and the list’s settings. Church lists default to showing ordinary members requests posted since their current membership began. Authorized church administrators and request authors may have additional access within those church access rules. Ordinary personal shared lists have separate sharing and history settings and default to including earlier active and answered requests. Their owners may change the history setting and have broader access; authors may view their own requests as those access rules allow. If an ordinary personal shared list is public, anyone on the internet can read its active and answered requests without membership or a SimplyPray account. Public material may be copied or indexed outside our control.

Ordinary personal shared-list audiences can change when membership, visibility or history settings change. Their invitation links can be forwarded and are subject to expiration and use limits. A person with a usable personal shared-list invitation may join under its settings; open-to-join personal shared lists may also allow joining separately from an invitation. Church-membership invitations are separate from these personal shared-list controls. Church lists remain private and subject to the active membership and qualifying church-sponsored access requirements above. Review the applicable audience before posting. Do not treat shared content as confidential, privileged or accessible only to a fixed audience.

Before syncing or sharing information about another person, obtain any permission required by law and limit the information to what is appropriate for that purpose and any intended audience. Enabling sync uploads existing personal content as well as later changes, so review sensitive information before enabling it. Use general descriptions or omit identifying details for sensitive matters. Another person’s health, religious beliefs or family situation can be sensitive even if included in a prayer request.

Recipients may copy, save, forward or capture content outside SimplyPray. Removing a post or account cannot retrieve those independent copies. A church may have its own legal responsibilities and privacy practices for information it receives.

6 Review and moderation of shared content

Shared requests are stored as readable text and are not end-to-end encrypted. Access rules and hosting safeguards protect them. Authorized personnel and providers may access them for service operation, support, security, moderation or legal compliance. Church administrator access is described in Section 5. Authorized church administrators may remove prayer requests from their church lists, subject to applicable law. Removal hides a request from ordinary list views and is separate from permanent deletion. A removed request can remain available to authorized reviewers during a 30-day review and restoration window. After 30 days from removal, it cannot be read or restored through the service; scheduled daily cleanup deletes the expired request from live storage. If cleanup is delayed, the access cutoff still applies. Separate safety records and backups are addressed in Section 10. Rights in submitted content are governed by applicable law. We do not promise continuous monitoring or emergency response. Report concerns to privacy@simplypray.io without unnecessary sensitive details.

10 Retention

Private prayers, journals, saved tags, devotional progress and settings remain on your device until you delete them or the applicable local data is erased. The approximately 90-day cleanup in Section 2 applies only to the local devotional activity log. Signing out does not erase your private records.

If you enable iCloud Sync on a supported Apple device, the iCloud copy remains when you turn sync off. Permanent deletion of a synced item is communicated to other devices when they next sync; archiving or hiding an item is not permanent deletion. The sync copy retains deletion markers with record identifiers and deletion/change information, rather than deleted prayer or journal text, to prevent older devices from restoring deleted records. Account deletion may leave an account-deletion marker for the same purpose. Cloud cleanup and copies on other devices are addressed in Section 11.

Online account information, active and answered shared requests, memberships and agreement receipts are retained while needed to provide the service, subject to deletion requests and applicable law. Removing a shared request starts the 30-day review and restoration window described in Section 6; that is not a 30-day limit for all shared prayers. Permanent deletion and account deletion are separate actions.

The church’s 60-day activity indicator is a reporting window, not a promise to erase all church or account information after 60 days. The 180-day website analytics choice and cookie limit in Section 9 is a browser-storage limit, not a retention period for information already received by the analytics provider.

Support messages, privacy-request records, safety reports and audit records, security and operational logs, and provider-cleanup records are kept for the time needed to resolve the matter, operate and secure the service, complete and verify deletion work, or meet a specifically justified legal or dispute-related need. These categories do not share a single fixed retention period. We limit retained information and access according to its purpose and delete or minimize it when that purpose no longer requires it, subject to applicable law. Some older moderation notes may require individual review to identify personal information and determine whether it must be removed.

Some billing and church audit records may remain after account deletion for lawful financial, security, legal or dispute-related purposes. These records may still identify you even when the direct link to your account is removed. We limit the information retained, access to it and how long it is kept according to the applicable purpose. Independent marketplace and payment providers may retain transaction records to meet their own legal obligations; those records are separate from information providers process on our behalf.

Some information may remain temporarily in protected backups until the applicable backup retention period expires or the deletion process completes. Backup periods depend on the service and configured recovery arrangement; they are separate from removal of records from live storage. Backup copies are restricted to recovery and other justified purposes, and applicable deletion requirements must be honored when restoring data. Device-provider backups and independent recipient copies are outside our direct control. Contact privacy@simplypray.io for information about the retention or deletion of a particular record.

11 Account and content deletion

You can initiate account deletion in the app’s account settings while online, or request deletion without reinstalling the app by emailing privacy@simplypray.io. Use the subject “Delete my SimplyPray account” and identify your account email address; do not send your password or unnecessary prayer details. We may reasonably verify your identity before acting. The server deletion removes your account, the shared requests you submitted and associated memberships, invitations and access grants, subject to the retained records described in Section 10. If the server request fails, local deletion does not proceed and you can retry.

We handle account-deletion requests and instruct providers processing personal information on our behalf as required by applicable law, subject to lawful retention exceptions. You do not need to submit a separate request to each of those providers. We provide responses, extension notices and explanations of retained information to the extent applicable law requires. Deletion from SimplyPray’s live account service and cleanup at subscription or payment providers are separate steps. Provider cleanup may remain queued or held for verification, safe handling of billing history, or resolution of a provider error; account deletion does not establish that every provider record has already been erased. We remain responsible for handling your request and communicating any applicable delay or lawful retention. Deleting your SimplyPray account does not itself cancel an Apple or Google Play subscription. Cancel Apple purchases in your Apple subscription settings and Google Play purchases at https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions while signed in to the purchasing Google account. Changing devices, uninstalling the app or joining a church does not itself cancel either subscription. For website-billed subscriptions, do not rely on account deletion as confirmation of cancellation; charges may continue while provider work is pending. Use the available billing controls or contact help@simplypray.io for assistance.

Shared lists and other people’s requests may remain after your account is deleted. Ordinary shared lists follow their ownership controls; church lists remain under church-authorized administration. A church’s wish to preserve history does not override your applicable privacy or deletion rights.

To leave church membership or sponsorship, contact your church administrator or help@simplypray.io. Leaving an ordinary shared list is a separate action. Leaving does not automatically delete earlier posts. Leaving the church ends your membership-based access to its private lists, including access based on authorship. Loss of qualifying church-sponsored access also ends access to those church lists; a personal subscription alone does not preserve it. Public access and author access on ordinary personal shared lists follow their separate access rules. Independent recipient copies may remain. For help deleting a request, including after you lose access, contact privacy@simplypray.io. Canceling a subscription, signing out, uninstalling and deleting an account are separate actions.

After server deletion succeeds, the app attempts to clear the deleted account’s private records, associated devotional progress and stored agreement proof on the device where deletion is initiated, and cancels its scheduled reminders. If local cleanup fails, the app records that cleanup remains pending and retries it; server success alone does not confirm that every local file was erased. It does not remotely erase every device or independently managed backup. If you request deletion by email or from another device, remove local app data and manage backups on your other devices as appropriate.

For supported Apple devices, if the device handling account deletion has the saved association with your iCloud sync account, the app queues cleanup of that account’s synced content. Cleanup requires an internet connection and access to the original iCloud account and is retried when the app is opened. Completion of server account deletion does not confirm that the iCloud copy has already been removed. Deletion by email or from a device that lacks the saved sync association may require additional assistance and access to the original sync device and iCloud account. Uninstalling or erasing a device before queued cleanup finishes can interrupt that cleanup. Cloud cleanup does not remotely erase local copies on your other devices.

You may also manage app data using Apple’s available iCloud storage controls. Turn SimplyPray sync off on every device before removing a cloud copy this way, so another device does not upload its local records again. Apple’s controls apply to app data in that iCloud account and may cover more than one SimplyPray account used with it. For help with an iCloud copy, incomplete cleanup or an account-deletion request, contact privacy@simplypray.io. We remain responsible for handling requests, explaining applicable delays or lawful retention, and meeting applicable deletion obligations; these technical dependencies do not limit your privacy rights.
