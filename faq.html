Frequently asked questions

Tap a question to read the answer. For a problem that needs personal help, visit Contact Support.

How do I add a prayer request?

On Home, tap Add a Prayer Request. Enter a title, add any details, choose a category and list, then tap Save Request. You can also tap the + beside a personal list to add a request directly to it.

How do I edit a personal prayer request?

Open Lists and tap a personal list to expand it. Tap the request, make your changes, then tap Save.

How do I remove a personal prayer request?

Expand its list and swipe left on the request to Archive it. Archiving removes it from active prayer while keeping it saved for restoration. You can also press and hold the request and choose Archive.

What if I archive something by mistake?

Tap Undo while the message is visible. If it has disappeared, restore the request from More → Archived Items.

How do I restore an archived item?

Open More → Archived Items, find the item, and tap Restore. Use the Show filter to find prayer requests, Thanksgiving entries, or answered prayers. A restored prayer request returns to its original list if it still exists; otherwise it appears under Ungrouped Prayers. Restoring an archived answered prayer brings it back to Answered Prayers & Praise, rather than active prayer.

How do I delete a personal prayer list?

On Lists, press and hold the personal list’s name, then choose Delete List. Choose Ungroup Items to keep its requests active under Ungrouped Prayers, or Archive Items to hide them from active prayer. If the list has no requests, Ungroup Items also deletes the empty list.

How do I mark a personal prayer as answered?

Expand a personal list and swipe right on the request. Add an optional note and confirm Mark Answered. You can also open the request and choose Mark as Answered. The request appears in Answered Prayers & Praise on Home, and a linked Thanksgiving entry is created.

What if I marked a prayer answered by mistake?

On Home, open Answered Prayers & Praise. Swipe on the answered prayer to reveal its actions, then choose Return to Prayer List. Before confirming, choose whether to keep its linked Thanksgiving entry. If you do not keep it, the Thanksgiving entry moves to Archived Items and can be restored later.

How do I add a request to a shared list?

Open the shared list from Lists and tap Add Request. Enter the request and any details, then tap Add. If Add Request is missing, the list’s settings may allow only administrators to add requests.

How do I invite someone to a shared list?

If you are a list administrator, open the shared list and tap the gear for List Settings. Under Members, tap Invite, then choose QR Code or Email Invite. Church-list membership is managed through the church subscription instead.

Who can see a shared list?

Shared lists are for their members. An administrator can enable Anyone with the link can join, so share invitations thoughtfully. Church prayer lists are private to the church subscription and include active church members automatically.

Why are some shared-list actions unavailable?

Permissions depend on your role and the list’s settings. Administrators choose who can add requests and who can mark them answered. Members can edit their own active, pending, or answered requests while they belong to the list. If you need an unavailable action, contact a list administrator.

How do I remove or delete a shared request?

Where permitted, a request shows Delete permanently. Confirming this deletes it for everyone on the list and cannot be undone. Copies saved elsewhere are not recalled.

Administrators can also choose Remove from list. This hides the request from the shared list and any public page, while retaining it for moderation. Removed requests do not use the personal Archived Items restoration flow. Once a request is removed, only its author can permanently delete it while still a member.

Where do answered shared requests go?

Open the shared list’s List Settings and look under Answered. When permitted by the list’s settings, swipe right on a shared request to mark it answered, or press and hold it and choose Mark answered.

How do I leave or delete a shared list?

Open the shared list’s List Settings and choose Leave list, where available. Leaving removes your access without deleting the list for everyone else. If you are the only administrator and other members remain, transfer ownership before leaving.

Administrators can delete a shared list through List Settings. Deleting it removes the list and its requests for everyone and cannot be undone. Church membership is managed separately; contact a church administrator about changes.

Does the app work offline?

You can add, edit, and pray through your personal prayer requests on your device without an internet connection. Shared lists need an internet connection to load the latest information and save changes. Account sign-in and subscription management also need an internet connection.

Where is my prayer data stored?

Personal prayer lists, Thanksgiving entries, answered prayers, and tags are stored on your device. Shared lists and their requests are stored in the cloud so members can access them. SimplyPray does not currently sync personal prayer data between devices.

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot Password? and enter your email address. Tap Send Reset Link, then check your email for the reset link. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive. If you are still unable to sign in, use Contact Support.

How do I manage or cancel an Apple subscription?

Open More → Account & Billing → Manage Apple subscriptions. You can also open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions → SimplyPray. Apple manages subscriptions purchased through the App Store.

How do I manage a web subscription?

Open More → Account & Billing → Manage web account and billing. Sign in to the web account associated with your subscription to manage its billing. Apple subscriptions are managed separately with Apple.

I was charged but access is missing. What should I do?

Check that you are signed in to the SimplyPray account associated with your purchase. For an App Store purchase, open More → Account & Billing and tap Restore purchases. If access is still missing, use Contact Support and include your account email, purchase date, and where you subscribed. Do not send passwords or payment card details.

How do I delete my account?

Open More → Delete Account and follow the confirmation prompts and account checks. Account deletion cannot be undone and clears that account’s local prayer data on this device. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage your billing separately before deleting the account.
