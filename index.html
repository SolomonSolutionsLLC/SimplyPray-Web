Why SimplyPray

Most prayer lives in your head, so most of it slips away.

Requests get forgotten. Routines fizzle by Wednesday. The answers God gives go unrecorded, so you forget how faithful He's been. SimplyPray gives prayer a shape you can keep: a guided rhythm, organized lists, and a living record of answered prayer you'll return to for years.

The ACTS Framework

A time-tested path
through prayer

Four movements that bring structure, Scripture, and depth to every prayer session, in 15 minutes or less.

A
Step 1
Adoration

Begin with who God is. Scripture-backed attributes guide you in praising God for His character, not just His gifts.

C
Step 2
Confession

Guided reflection prompts honest naming of sin, and the freedom of bringing it into the light before God.

T
Step 3
Thanksgiving

Name God's faithfulness. Record answered prayers and build a living record of His goodness you can return to for years.

S
Step 4
Supplication

Organized lists for family, church, work, and more. Tag, filter, and pray through what matters most. Nothing falls through.
